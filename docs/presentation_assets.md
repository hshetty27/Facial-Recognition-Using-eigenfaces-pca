# Presentation Assets

All figures are generated by code. They are listed here in the order of the mathematical story.

- **AT&T figures:** regenerate all of them with `python -m experiments.run_all`. They are saved in `outputs/figures/`.
- **Demo figures:** made by `python demo.py ...` and saved in `outputs/demo/`. They come from whichever dataset was last trained (currently the 4-person AT&T rehearsal sample).

Sizes are in pixels at 120 dpi.

## Numbers for slides

Source files: `outputs/results/pca_summary.txt` and `recognition_summary.txt`.

- **Dimensions:** d = 4992, N_train = 280, N_test = 120. U is 4992 × 280, rank(X_c) = 279.
- **Variance:** λ₁ = 22.06 (19.6 % of the variance); total variance = 112.37.
- **Best recognition:** 98.3 % (118/120) at k = 30, with 80.9 % of the variance retained.
- **Numerical checks:** ‖X_c − UΣVᵀ‖/‖X_c‖ ≈ 2 × 10⁻¹⁵; max |UᵀU − I| ≈ 3 × 10⁻¹⁵.
